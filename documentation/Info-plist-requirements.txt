Required Info.plist Entries for Live Speech-to-Text
===================================================

Add these entries to your Info.plist file (or in Xcode's Info tab):

1. Microphone Access:
   Key: NSMicrophoneUsageDescription
   Value: This app needs microphone access to transcribe your meetings in real-time.

2. Speech Recognition:
   Key: NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription  
   Value: This app uses speech recognition to transcribe your meetings as you speak.

3. Screen Recording (already may be present):
   Key: NSScreenCaptureUsageDescription
   Value: This app records your screen to capture meeting content.


How to add in Xcode:
--------------------
1. Select your project in the Project Navigator
2. Select your target
3. Go to the "Info" tab
4. Click the "+" button to add a new key
5. Start typing the key name (e.g., "NSMicrophoneUsageDescription")
6. Xcode will autocomplete it
7. Enter the description string in the Value field


XML format (if editing Info.plist directly):
--------------------------------------------
<key>NSMicrophoneUsageDescription</key>
<string>This app needs microphone access to transcribe your meetings in real-time.</string>

<key>NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription</key>
<string>This app uses speech recognition to transcribe your meetings as you speak.</string>

<key>NSScreenCaptureUsageDescription</key>
<string>This app records your screen to capture meeting content.</string>
